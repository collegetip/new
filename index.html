📜 NAMC Tip Community Rules

1. Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Abuse

Insults, profanity, bullying, defamation, and targeted harassment against students, doctors, or staff are strictly prohibited. Abusive words will be automatically scrubbed.

2. Strict Privacy Protection (No Doxxing)

Never share personal mobile numbers, emails, roll numbers, or personal room details. The system automatically redacts sensitive contact identifiers.

3. Keep Tips Constructive & Academic

Share useful guidance for viva preparation, clinical rounds, exam questions, study resources, and genuine hostel hacks that help fellow peers succeed.

4. Community Moderation

Any student can report inappropriate tips using the flag button. Posts flagged multiple times will be prioritized for permanent deletion by the admin.
